Replies and comments

Replies shown under posts were written by visitors on the original website. This copy is static: it does not allow replying, liking or subscribing. To take part, go to the original post.

Audio and video

Audio and video files are not stored in this copy. They are played from the original website, so they work only while you are online and while the original website stays available. The collective has said the original website will remain live for the coming year; after that, these recordings may stop playing. Wherever this applies, a note with a link to the original post appears next to the player.
